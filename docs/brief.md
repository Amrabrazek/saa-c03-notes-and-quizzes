# Brief

**What:** a free website with study notes, diagrams, 20-question quizzes and interactive drills for the AWS Solutions Architect – Associate (SAA-C03) exam.

**Why:** it started as Amr's 3,000-line personal study guide. Topics he kept re-adding were his weak spots, and some statements were wrong or out of date. The site turns that into corrected, exam-first notes plus practice that targets those exact confusions. It's shared publicly to help other people preparing for the exam.

**Who for:** SAA-C03 candidates who learn best from diagrams, comparisons and short explanations.

**Shape:** 10 sections grouped by what the exam confuses (Edge & DNS, VPC, Hybrid, S3, ...), a weak-spots page, and later a cross-section "mix-ups" page.

**Non-goals:** covering every AWS service; replacing official AWS docs; accounts or a backend.
